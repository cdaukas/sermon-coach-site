## What a report of tonight's quality actually requires

Reading back what worked, the report needs four things, and the third is the one that fails quietly.

**Three or four countable measures**, so the strengths and focus sections have something to rank.

**A quotable span per measure.** An ask, a sentence, a clause. This is what carries the page. A measure that counts a whole-document property has nothing to put in a quote block.

**A rewrite target.** The Was/Now only works when the failure lives in one sentence that can be rewritten to fix its marker. A missing conclusion cannot be rewritten in a line.

**Interpretation material.** A finding worth a paragraph that names the instinct underneath the habit, not just the number.

## The inventory

### Criterion 2 · Christ-centered arc — RICHEST

**Measures.** Agency share (Christ as grammatical subject of a finite action verb, machine r ≈ +0.91 twice, κ = 1.000 across 520 spans). Christ in a numbered main point (census: 21 of 4,227). Locus count and locus spread (I2, +0.539, +0.518). Christ density.

**Quotable.** Yes, and cleanly. Every SUBJ span is a sentence. *"He punished Jesus for every wicked sin of His people, He did not relent until it was finished."*

**Rewrite target.** Excellent. Turning a genitive mention into an action is a one-sentence fix.

**Interpretation.** Strong. One outline point in two hundred, corpus-wide. Within a genre, how much you talk about Christ tells you nothing about whether he acts. **And the genre caveat has to be printed:** gospel narrative runs roughly double wisdom, F = 5.58.

**Verdict: build this second.** Best-validated instruments in the project and three or four measures.

---

### Criterion 3 · Gospel clarity — RICH

**Measures.** The outline test (7:1). Named-object construction (φ, r ≈ +0.29, indicator not discriminator). Passion-narrative detail density. Non-Christian address, presence and position.

**Quotable.** Yes, and the contrast is vivid. *"He punished Jesus for every wicked sin of His people"* against *"for our sins"* with nothing given to it.

**Rewrite target.** Excellent. Naming the object of the cross is exactly a one-sentence rewrite.

**Interpretation.** Strong, and one line will land hard: across 443 sermons, **exactly one asks a non-believer to take a physical step.** Median address position is 88 to 91% through the manuscript.

**Do not attempt load-bearing versus removable.** Four instruments, best r = +0.22. No surface signature.

**Verdict: build this third.**

### Criterion 9 · Pastoral specificity — RICH, and already built

Three measures, all countable, all quotable, all with working rewrites. Tonight's report.

**One addition available:** the two-marker screen. A fact known only by report or observation, plus an ask naming a cost that falls on some and not others. **One-way screen only** — passing is strong evidence, failing means nothing.

---

### Criterion 4 · Fallen Condition Focus — WORKABLE

**Measures.** Whose condition it is: the room, the text's original audience, or nobody. Explicit versus implied. Position. Second-person negative in the opening fifth (r = +0.206, +0.286, the only leg that held twice).

**Quotable.** Yes. The condition sentence is a sentence.

**Rewrite target.** Good. Turning an ownerless condition into a hearer-owned one is a one-sentence fix, and it is the criterion's actual failure mode.

**Interpretation.** Strong. The ownerless condition at 30 to 42%, not the biblical-audience one, which is 2 in 60 twice. The coupling at 80% and 77%. And the structural line: `PROP:` in 88 files, `BIG IDEA` in 88, **`FCF:` in 6.**

**Caution.** Three of four instrument legs reversed sign between samples. Build on the one that held; claim nothing more.

---

### Criterion 10 · Ecclesial faithfulness — WORKABLE

**Measures.** RI, reciprocal object near an imperative (pooled ≈ +0.56). Churchword density. Edge-share (church language at ~2× chance in the first or last 12%). EFI ≥ 4 as a positive-only signal, precision 1.00 twice with recall 0.15.

**Quotable.** Yes. Tonight's focus 3 proves it.

**Rewrite target.** Proven tonight, and it produced the best rewrite on the card.

**Interpretation.** Strong. The plurals that do not stay plural: a commentator quoted arguing an imperative is corporate, the argument endorsed, three private disciplines handed out. And the finding nobody asks for: **a hearer who arrives late or leaves early hears a materially less ecclesial sermon.**

**Do not credit calendared occasions.** Flat to half a percentage point across 93 manuscripts.

---

### Criterion 11 · Expository exultation — WORKABLE, one strong measure

**Measure.** The grain tell: does the delight name a specific textual object. **φ = +0.715 then +0.726, κ = 0.908, the most reliable hand judgement in the project.** And it works on a single sermon, which nothing else does.

**Quotable.** Yes, natively. The exulting span is the quote.

**Rewrite target.** Good. Adding the named object is a one-sentence fix.

**Interpretation.** Strong, and it has a shape nothing else has: **the object migrates.** First tenth, TEXT .21 and DOCTRINE .43. Final tenth, TEXT .10 and DOCTRINE **.78.** He begins enjoying the passage and ends enjoying what it supports.

**Do not score emphasis markers.** Exclamation, capitals, interjections carry no information. Failed twice, pre-registered to fail both times.

**Thin on measure count, rich on interpretation.** One measure plus the migration.

---

### Criterion 1 · Textual fidelity — ONE MEASURE, NO COUNTER

**Measure.** The correction move: **28 of 28 top-tier manuscripts across two blind pre-registered runs.** Nothing else in eleven criteria hits 28 of 28.

**Quotable.** Yes, and it is the best quote material in the project. *"YOU WOULD THINK THAT THE NEXT THING HE WOULD SAY IS... GIVE THEM MORE LAW! But it doesn't say that — it actually says the opposite."*

**Rewrite target.** Awkward. You cannot rewrite a sentence into a correction move; the move requires that the text actually surprised the preacher. **This measure gets strength quotes, not Was/Now.**

**Interpretation.** Strong, including the finding that criterion 1's production scoring does not discriminate.

**Blocked.** No counter, and it needs the hand audit first. A regex catches barely half.

---

### Criterion 5 · Structure — WORKABLE, WRONG LENS

**Measures.** TRD (ρ ≈ −0.42 twice). L6 (+0.30, +0.51). Frame-break. Point count. Controlling image, **which measures nothing** — 59% of derived sermons and 54% of imposed.

**Quotable.** Different in kind. No ask to quote. Evidence is the point heads in a column, or the final two sentences.

**Rewrite target.** None. A conclusion is not rewritten in a line.

**Interpretation.** The strongest in the project: **48 to 10, McNemar p < 0.001, four independent draws.**

**Two constraints.** Manuscript-only — a transcript cannot fail a structure test. And these are **prep-lens** instruments: they measure whether the preacher stopped writing, not whether he ended badly.

**Verdict:** a real report of a different shape. Better suited to the prep card than to a quarterly deep dive.

---

### Criterion 6 · Hard things handled — NO MEASURE, RICH MATERIAL

**What exists.** An eight-plus-three taxonomy of softening moves: narrow or redefine, the immediate pivot, announce the difficulty, relocate to them, pre-empt the objection, the borrowed voice, the skipped scene, generalize to "we." Unresolved hard things: **336 instances across all 66 manuscripts in the replication set.**

**Quotable.** Yes, richly. Both a softening move and an abandoned hard thing are quotable spans.

**Rewrite target.** Plausible but unproven. Rewriting a softened passage to face the difficulty is a bigger move than fixing a marker.

**Interpretation.** Strong, and one item belongs on the card unconditionally: **credit self-aimed humor, φ ≈ 0.73 across 1,757 instances. Do not attempt to flag trivializing** — both predictors reversed sign.

**What is needed:** a counter for softening moves and for abandoned hard things, validated against a hand list.

---

### Criterion 7 · Application to present audience — THIN

**The problem.** The application corpus work is concreteness, which is criterion 9. Criterion 7 is present-tense landing and Keller's three audiences, and almost nothing in the derivation speaks to it directly.

**What exists.** The non-Christian address data, which criterion 3 also claims. That is one measure at best, and it is borrowed.

**Verdict: needs its own corpus read.** The three-audiences question — believers, doubters, seekers — was never asked of the corpus.

---

### Criterion 8 · Emotional arc — NO MEASURE, AND A NEGATIVE RESULT

**What exists.** Peak dominance, concentration, immersive share. All replicate to three decimals across two coder panels.

**But.** Between-coder variance is 47.3% then 32.8% of density. **Peak location is dead** at inter-coder +0.096. Both of run 1's instruments failed, one reversing sign. And the criterion is roughly calibrated already: the corpus is genuinely flat, one manuscript in sixteen architecturally shaped.

**Quotable.** Poorly. An affective event is a span, but the interesting quantity is a relation across the whole manuscript.

**Verdict: the thinnest of the eleven, and it may be correct to say so.** A report that tells a preacher his emotional arc is flat like almost everyone's, with no reliable per-sermon evidence, is a report worth not shipping.

## The decisions this forces

**1. Is the rotation by criterion or by measure?** Tonight's report ranked measures across three criteria and it worked. A criterion rotation is a different product and thins out fast: five criteria have one measure or none.

**2. Where do strengths come from?** If focus is scoped to the quarter's criterion, strengths should still be drawn from all twelve measures. A strength does not have to be about the same criterion to be true, and it keeps every quarter substantial.

**3. What happens to criteria 5 and 8?** Structure is prep-lens and has no rewrite. Emotional arc has no reliable per-sermon evidence. **Both may be right to exclude from the rotation** rather than padded into it.

**4. Criterion 7 has no material.** It needs the corpus read the other ten already had, framed on the three audiences.

**5. Criterion 1 is one hand audit away** from being one of the strongest reports available, because the correction move has the best quote material in the entire project.
