# Two artifacts, not one

The quarterly report and the prep card. Product spec.

2 September 2026. Supersedes the single-artifact framing in `claude/spec-prep-card.md`. Copy in `claude/prep-card-copy-library.md`. Evidence in `claude/claude_corpus-derivation.md`.

---

## Why two

The prep card and the quarterly report were being designed as one document and they are not one job.

**The quarterly report** is read once, sitting down, five to seven pages. It carries the evidence, the interpretation, and the one deep observation. It is what a preacher forwards to his elders.

**The prep card** is one page and it stays on the desk. Three strengths, three questions, one gate. It is used while writing, not read.

Merging them produces a document too long to keep open on a Saturday and too thin to be worth a quarter's wait.

**The loop:** report → three priorities → card carries those three → next report opens by saying whether they moved.

## Part 1: The quarterly report

### Structure, in order

**1. At a glance.** Sermons reviewed, the date range, and **one sentence** on where the quarter sits.

> Twenty-three sermons, June through August. Your preaching shows strong textual engagement and pastoral warmth. The clearest opportunity is moving from good explanation toward application someone could recognize in their life.

**No tallies of strengths and focus areas.** "Seven recurring strengths" implies the report found seven and the card carries three. **No overall score, no band, no grade.**

**2. What is working.** Three to four, each with observation, count, and one line of interpretation.

**3. Where to focus.** Three, each with observation, count, **what this suggests**, and the coaching question.

**4. The pattern behind the pattern.** One per quarter. See below.

**5. Don't lose this.** Two or three things explicitly not to change.

**6. Your focus for next quarter.** The three priorities, each with a goal stated as a count that will move.

**7. Before you preach.** The five questions, reproduced as the bridge to the card.

**8. Closing.** The pastoral turn.

### The addition that matters most: "What this suggests"

A count with an interpretation under it is a different artifact from a count alone. **This is where the coach earns his fee.**

```
Make obedience visible

When you call people to respond, ask whether someone could recognize
the obedience in their life.

5 of 23 sermons.

What this suggests: you are strong at conviction and direction. The
opportunity is translating that conviction into something a person
could be seen doing this week.

Ask yourself: What exactly am I asking people to do?
```

**Constraint.** The interpretation is **fixed text per measure**, written once, selected by rank. The model does not write it. This is the rule that stopped the deep dive inventing a finding nothing had measured.

### The pattern behind the pattern

**One per quarter.** Not a count. A sentence that names the shape underneath the three focus areas.

> You are much better at telling people what is true than telling them what faithfulness looks like.

> You consistently mention Christ, but Christ rarely drives where the sermon goes.

**This is the most distinctive thing the product can offer** and it is the closest artifact to the archetype read, which landed correctly on two preachers with opposite failure modes.

**Three constraints, and they are not optional.**

It must be **accountable to the counts already on the page**. If it makes a claim, the evidence sits above it. A metaphor that predicts nothing is a horoscope.

It is **written from a fixed library keyed to the combination of the three focus areas**, not generated freely. There are a manageable number of combinations and each gets a sentence written once.

**One per quarter, never two.** Its power is that it is the only sentence of its kind in the document.

### What the report must not do

**No percentages.** "18 of 23 · 78%" is worse than "18 of 23." At these sample sizes a binary rate near 50% carries an eleven-point standard error, and the percentage implies precision that is not there. **The count is the honest form.**

**No trend tables.** A row reading 82, 87, 91, 94 reads as steady improvement and would mostly be noise across 23-sermon quarters. **Report movement only when it clears a threshold, and say plainly when it has not moved.**

> Last quarter you worked on naming the cost. It was 1 of 17. It is
> now 4 of 19.

> Last quarter you worked on naming the cost. It was 1 of 17 and it is
> 1 of 19. It has not moved.

**No profile bars.** Six dimensions with filled bars and Strong / Developing labels is a score with the number filed off. It also puts textual fidelity on a scale where **production is confirmed not to discriminate** — a 4 on 80% of sermons against a hand-coded 40–59% mode. The bar would look confident about the thing we know is broken. See defect D1.

**No overall summary number.** The moment one appears the report becomes an evaluation.

## Part 2: The prep card

**One page. It stays on the desk.**

### Structure

**1. Three strengths**, from all twelve measures. Claim, one line, count. No questions; nothing to do.

**2. Three focus areas**, from the actionable seven. Claim, one line, count, and the question.

**3. The reverence check.** Never ranked, always present.

**4. Footer.** Sample size and date.

**Strengths first.** He reads the work list last, so the final thing in front of him is what he is doing this Saturday.

**Cut from the card, kept in the report:** the preamble, "what this suggests," the pattern behind the pattern, the closing. The card is not a short report; it is a different thing.

### The five questions

The card's three questions are drawn from a fixed set of five, which appear in full at the end of the report:

1. What did this text make me see that I would not have seen on my own?
2. What exactly am I asking people to do?
3. What will obedience to this text cost them?
4. What is Christ doing in this text, and where does that belong in my outline?
5. Could my application be obeyed entirely by myself?

**The report shows all five. The card shows the three that are his.**

## Pastoral wisdom

The reverence check is the first entry in a category that is not scoreable and should not be.

> On any text of judgment, wrath, or suffering: would this joke or clever aside survive if the grieving person in row three were the only listener?

**Both candidate predictors of trivializing humor reversed sign between corpus reads.** It is not measurable and should not be attempted. It is a question a preacher asks himself, and it is exactly the kind of observation that makes the report read as though it came from someone who understands preaching.

**The category can grow, but only with items that are genuinely unmeasurable and genuinely wise.** It is not a holding pen for findings that failed validation.

## Open

1. **The pattern-behind-the-pattern library.** How many combinations of three focus areas produce a distinct sentence, and who writes them. This is the highest-value copy in either artifact and it does not exist.
2. **Movement threshold.** What counts as moved. Provisional: a change of at least a third of the baseline, or at least three sermons, whichever is larger. Should come from the norms run, which is parked on privacy.
3. **Discipline 1 has no counter.** The correction move is 28 of 28 in the corpus reads and needs the hand-audit validation the object marker got before it can be ranked.
4. **Cadence.** Calendar quarter or every twelve sermons. A guest preacher at six a year waits two years for a second report.
